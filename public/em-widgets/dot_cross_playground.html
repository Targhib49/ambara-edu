Dot product measures overlap. Cross product measures spread.

Drag either arrowhead. Watch which quantity dies when the vectors line up, and which dies when they square off.

The two zero-conditions are opposites. Drag the arrows until they lie on top of each other: the shaded area collapses and the cross product goes to zero, while the dot product hits its maximum. Now drag them square to each other: the dot product goes to zero and the area is at its largest. Students swap these two tests constantly — dot zero means perpendicular, cross zero means parallel. Press “Swap to B × A” and note that the number changes sign while the dot product does not.
