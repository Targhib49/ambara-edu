The bridge relation

agree?
Three things to try. (1) Set θ = 90°. The point drops into the xy-plane and ρ becomes equal to r — the only place those two ever coincide. (2) Drag θ toward 0°. The point climbs to the pole and ρ collapses to nothing, which is exactly why the φ arc length carries a factor sin θ. (3) Push φ past 90° and watch the Cartesian x go negative. That is the situation your calculator's arctan cannot represent, and the red warning tells you when you are in it.
