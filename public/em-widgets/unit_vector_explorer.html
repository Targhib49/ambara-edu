What to notice. At φ = 0 the arrow labelled aρ points along +x. At φ = 90° the same label points along +y. It is a different arrow wearing the same name — which is exactly why aρ cannot be pulled out of an integral over φ. Turn on the running sum and go to 360°: the total is zero, because every outward arrow is cancelled by the one diametrically opposite. Stop at 180° instead and the sum is 2ay — the cancellation is a property of the complete period, not of aρ itself.
